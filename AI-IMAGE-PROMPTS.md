# AI image prompts for GHY Pro

Use these in ChatGPT (image generation), Gemini, Midjourney, Ideogram or similar.
Save each result with the **exact filename** shown and drop it in the folder given.
It replaces the current photo everywhere on the site; no code changes needed.

- Product photos → `public/products/<filename>`: square or 4:3, at least 1200 px wide, JPG.
- Scene photos → `public/images/<filename>`: sizes listed per image.
- After replacing a photo, delete its entry from `src/photoCredits.json` (the credits page lists only borrowed photos).

## Shared style (paste before every prompt)

> Professional food photography for a premium fresh-meat and fish delivery brand in Guwahati, Assam.
> Soft natural window light from the left, shallow depth of field, clean and appetising, true-to-life colours.
> Muted sage-green and warm wood props, matte ceramic, a little crushed ice where it suits raw fish.
> No text, no logos, no hands, no people. Photorealistic, high detail.
